Something about myself

I am 23 years old and currently a student at the University of Transilvanya, Faculty of Sociology and Communication, specializing in Digital Media. I previously studied at the Faculty of Electrical Engineering and Computer Science, but my interests did not align with that field, so I decided to pursue something more modern and relevant and agreed on Digital Media due to the growing impact of social media on society as well as the connection between people and it's potential to influence the future.

Through my studies in Digital Media, I've become more interested in how technology, communication, and cultures connect. I’m drawn to topics like online identity, online addiction and the implications of social media. These areas make me think about the content we consume and create, and how digital platforms shape our perspectives and behaviors. I see this field as an opportunity to understand media and people through what influences us, how we express ourselves, and how we connect in todays age.

Studying Electrical Engineering before Sociology opened the door to 3 main interests I didn’t expect to like, such as:

A fondness for math and physics

I've always liked math, but over time, I’ve come to like it even more. It’s not just about solving problems, it’s about understanding patterns and logic. Physics, on the other hand, I couldn't get into it. The concepts were tough to grasp, but as I kept at it, I started finding it more interesting than I expected. The way everything in the universe connects through physics really caught my attention.

A bigger interest in technology

It’s not just something I use, it’s something that really sparks my curiosity. The more I learn about technology, the more I realize how much there is to explore. There’s always something new happening, whether it’s a new innovation or a cool new app, I find it exciting to stay updated. It’s amazing to see how fast everything evolves, and I love diving into the changes and seeing how they shape the world around us.

Interest in sustainability

After learning about sustainability and the importance of choosing electronic devices that contribute to less CO2 emissions, I’ve really started to pay more attention to the environmental impact of my choices. It’s not just about picking the most energy efficient products, but about making more mindful decisions in general. From opting for devices with longer lifespans to choosing brands with stronger sustainability commitments, I try to incorporate these principles into my daily life.


Future Plans


Looking ahead, I plan to build a career that blends my technical curiosity with my interest for understanding people and digital culture. My current studies help me explore how technology impacts human behavior and communication. In the future, I hope to work in a field where I can contribute to meaningful social change. I’m especially interested in the influence of social media on society, and I’d love to be part of projects that help shape healthier online spaces or improve the way we interact with digital technologies or the way we interact with eachother. I want to keep learning and stay curious about the relationship between people and media.


As part of Gen Z, I’ve grown up surrounded by fast technological change and social media. This has shaped how I communicate and how I see the world. My generation is often seen as digital natives but we’re also deeply aware of the social issues that come with digital life, like misinformation, mental health challenges, and social media addiction. That’s why I chose to study Digital Media. I want to better understand how online platforms influence identity, relationships, and society. My goal is to work in a field where I can use this knowledge to create positive change, whether that’s through research, communication strategies, or media projects that raise awareness and challenge harmful norms. In the future, I hope to contribute to projects that reflect these values.
